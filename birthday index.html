A Love Letter for Your Birthday

Take your time and read this slowly, like I’m whispering each word in your ear.

My love,

Another year of your life, another year of me being lucky enough to be part of it. I still can’t believe that somehow, in this big, messy world, I get to call you my home.

You are my favorite notification, my softest place to land, and the most beautiful part of every single day. Your laugh makes everything feel lighter, your hugs make the loudest days go silent, and your heart… your heart is the gentlest thing I’ve ever known.

I love the tiny things you probably think I don’t notice – the way your eyes light up when you talk about what you love, the way you care so deeply for the people around you, the way you keep going even on the days that feel heavy. You are strong, you are kind, you are breathtakingly beautiful, and I am endlessly proud of you.

On your birthday, I just want you to remember this: you are enough, more than enough. You are worthy of every good thing, every soft moment, every dream coming true. If I could, I’d hang the stars a little lower tonight so you could reach them easier.

Thank you for choosing me, for trusting me with your heart, for letting me love you in all your shades of light and shadow. I promise to keep choosing you, over and over again, in every version of tomorrow.

I love you more than words can say, more than this little website can ever show, and more than my shy heart always manages to express.

Always, forever, and then a little more.
